**Description:**

4,200 years ago, a people from east of Mesopotamia toppled Akkad, the world's first empire, and ruled the cradle of civilization for a full century: the Gutians.

Sumerian scribes called them "the Dragon of the Mountains." According to the Sumerian King List, "they were their own kings." Leaders chosen by council, a confederation of tribes and local autonomy... Many historians count the Gutians among the oldest ancestors of the Kurds.

Drawing on Sumerian and Akkadian sources and on the work of scholars such as Arshak Safrastian, G. R. Driver, V. Minorsky, E. A. Speiser and Mehrdad Izady, this video tells the story of the Gutians.
